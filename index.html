House Rules

Scheduled service, not an on-demand app — here's how we keep it smooth.

✦

Book ahead. All rides need at least 12 hours notice. Quick Trips lock in by 8 PM the night before. I'm your scheduled driver, not a same-minute app.

✦

Deposits hold your slot. Night Out, Half-Day, Full-Day — and any ride quoted over $100 — are confirmed with a 50% deposit (Cash App, Apple Pay, or PayPal). No deposit, no reservation. Cancel 24+ hours out and it comes straight back.

✦

Cancellations. Free up to 24 hours before pickup. Inside 24 hours, the deposit stays with me. No-shows owe the full fare.

✦

Up to 4 riders. That's every seat with a belt. First two riders are included; riders three and four are +$5 each. Kids ride in a proper car seat that you provide — that's Texas law, no exceptions.

✦

Respect the car. No smoking or vaping. If the car needs professional cleaning after your trip, there's a $150 cleaning fee.

✦

Payment. Cash App $PayRollingHills, Apple Pay, PayPal (@OffEarthLLc), or cash — balance due at pickup. Tolls, parking, and airport fees added at cost. Hourly blocks end at the booked time.

✦

Coffee runs & errands. Need something grabbed before pickup? $10 errand fee plus the item cost, settled with your balance. Put the order in your booking.

✦

Plan your stops. Multi-stop night? List every address in the booking form and the whole route is locked before I arrive — no mid-ride guesswork.

✦

Driver's discretion. I can decline any ride where safety or respect is in question. Credits are prepayment, not priority — every booking still follows the notice rules and the calendar. If I ever end a booking relationship, your unused balance comes back to you in full.
